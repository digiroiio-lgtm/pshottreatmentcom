# Medical SEO, YMYL and AI Information Architecture Audit

Audit date: 2026-09-22  
Site: https://pshottreatment.com  
Repository baseline: `24515f9` on `copilot/add-high-conversion-blogs`  
Implementation branch: `codex/medical-seo-ia`

## Executive summary

The site was a crawlable Next.js application with useful ED topic coverage, but it mixed medical information with unsupported promotional claims, anonymous testimonials, unverified provider signals and sensational first-person framing. PRP/P-Shot evidence was often presented more strongly than current guideline positions support.

The implementation preserves every existing live URL and repositions the site around this sequence:

`ENTITY -> CONDITION -> TREATMENT -> EVIDENCE -> PROVIDER -> COST -> ASSESSMENT`

The release introduces a central route registry, evidence registry, calibrated claim language, source lists, evidence labels, explicit review-status disclosure, provenance-safe schema, deterministic sitemap dates, curated `llms.txt`, trust-policy pages and automated runtime validation.

No URL was removed, renamed, redirected, canonicalised elsewhere or marked `noindex`. This avoids destructive SEO changes without search-performance evidence.

## Information architecture and cannibalisation control

The ED Knowledge Hub owns condition discovery and established-care framing. Cause pages answer one diagnostic context each. The PRP evidence page owns efficacy and guideline status. Comparison pages answer explicit treatment comparisons. Provider and travel pages own verification and logistics. Cost pages retain distinct quotation, regional, anecdotal-source or inclusion-checklist intent. Contact owns assessment conversion.

Sensational URL slugs remain because ranking and backlink data were unavailable. Their H1s, direct answers and page bodies now declare the real purpose, reducing conflict without speculative redirects.

## GEO and AI retrieval readiness

- Direct answer paragraphs appear near each page H1.
- Evidence status is explicit: Established, Moderate, Limited, Uncertain or Not medical evidence.
- Claims separate proposed mechanism, observed trial outcome and guideline recommendation.
- Sources include publisher, year, evidence type and direct link.
- `llms.txt` states the site's scope, central evidence position and canonical source pages.
- Page schema avoids unsupported clinics, physicians, reviews, ratings and publication dates.
- Breadcrumbs and related-reading links expose the entity-to-assessment path.

## Human verification required before stronger commercial claims

The following information is not present in a verifiable form in the repository and must not be inferred:

1. Legal operator name and company details.
2. Treating facility name, complete address and licence or registration.
3. Treating clinician name, role, registration number and credential source.
4. Named medical reviewer and dated review record.
5. Exact PRP preparation and injection protocol.
6. Written consent, contraindication, emergency and aftercare documents.
7. Full price scope, refund terms and any optional charges.
8. Review provenance and permission to republish.
9. Before/after provenance, consent and a clinically meaningful outcome method.
10. Search Console query, impression, click, indexing and backlink data.

Once verified, these items should be added through the central data model and reviewed for visible-copy/schema consistency. Until then, the site intentionally discloses the limitation.

## Release decision

The implementation is technically ready to merge after repository review. It does not authorise publishing new clinician, clinic, review, rating or outcome claims. Any future URL consolidation should be based on Search Console, analytics and backlink evidence, followed by a mapped redirect and canonical plan.
